# This Week's Plan Card — Design Archive

Removed from the Today tab (was Sunday-only). Kept here for future reference.

## What it looked like
- Yellow gradient banner ("This Week's Plan 📋")
- Subtitle: "Here's what you need to do this week to stay on track."
- Each goal listed with:
  - Goal name (left)
  - "Nx this week" teal pill (right)
  - Day-of-week chips below (Mon, Tue, Wed... highlighted in teal)

## Style notes
- Yellow/teal colour contrast worked well
- Day chips were compact and scannable
- The "Nx this week" pill gave a quick frequency summary
- Overall: clear, dense but not cluttered — good at-a-glance weekly commitment view
